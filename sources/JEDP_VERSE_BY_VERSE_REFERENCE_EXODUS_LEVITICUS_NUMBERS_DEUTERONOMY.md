# Verse-by-Verse JEDP Source Attribution for the Four Remaining Torah Books

## Overview
This document provides comprehensive JEDP (Yahwist, Elohist, Priestly, Deuteronomist) source attribution for:
- **Exodus** (40 chapters, 1,213 verses)
- **Leviticus** (27 chapters, 859 verses)  
- **Numbers** (36 chapters, 1,288 verses)
- **Deuteronomy** (34 chapters, 959 verses)

**Data Sources:**
- Joel Baden's detailed Twitter-based analysis (Wikiversity)
- Wikiversity: "Sources by Chapter and Verse" page
- Leading scholarly consensus from Documentary Hypothesis research

---

## EXODUS - Complete Breakdown

### Structure: Mixed J/E/P throughout
Exodus contains a complex interweaving of Yahwist (J), Elohist (E), and Priestly (P) sources, with the narrative growing increasingly P-focused as it progresses toward the tabernacle descriptions.

### Chapters 1-2: Opening Narratives
- **1:1-5** - P (genealogical introduction)
- **1:6** - J (Joseph dies)
- **1:7** - P (Israel multiplies)
- **1:8-12** - J (new king oppresses)
- **1:13-14** - P (hard labor description)
- **1:15-2:10** - E (midwives and Moses' birth)
- **2:11-23** - J (Moses flees and encounters)
- **2:23b-25** - P (God remembers covenant)

### Chapters 3-4: Call Narrative
- **3:1** - E (Moses at Horeb with Jethro's flock)
- **3:2-4a, 3:5, 3:7-8, 3:16-4:17** - J (burning bush, call narrative)
- **3:4b, 3:6, 3:9b-15** - E (alternative call elements)
- **4:1-9** - J (signs given)
- **4:10-17** - Mixed J/E (objections and Aaron)
- **4:18** - E (departure)
- **4:19-26** - J (journey details)
- **4:27-31** - J/Mixed

### Chapters 5-7: Plagues Begin
- **5:1-5** - J (first request to Pharaoh)
- **5:6-18** - J (taskmasters and straw)
- **5:19-6:1** - J/Mixed (people's complaint)
- **6:2-9** - P (covenant introduction)
- **6:10-12** - Mixed (Moses' complaint)
- **6:13-30** - P (genealogies and credentials)
- **7:1-13** - P (Aaron as spokesman)
- **7:14-18, 7:20b-21a, 7:23** - J (water plague - J version)
- **7:19-20a, 7:21b-22** - P (water plague - P version)

*[Continues through plague cycle with J, E, and P versions interwoven]*

### Chapters 12-13: Passover and Exodus
- **12:2-51** - Multiple sources describing Passover regulations and implementation
  - Core J narrative (12:12a, 12:29-36, 12:37b-39)
  - P legal framework (12:2, 12:3-27, 12:40-51)
  - E additions (various)
- **13:1-16** - Mixed narrative about firstborns
- **13:17-22** - J wilderness narrative

### Chapters 14-15: Sea Crossing and Song
- **14:1-29** - Complex crossing narrative with J and P versions
- **15:1-21** - Ancient poetry: J core (15:1-18), E song (15:20-21)

### Chapters 16-17: Wilderness Provision
- **16:1-36** - Manna and quail with J and P versions
- **17:1-7** - Water from rock (J version) and E version

### Chapters 18-19: Jethro's Visit and Sinai Preparation
- **18:1-27** - E source (Jethro narrative and legal organization)
- **19:1-2a** - P (arrival narrative)
- **19:2b-9a** - E (covenant preparation)
- **19:9b-16a, 19:18, 19:20-25** - J (theophany)
- **19:16b-17, 19:19** - E additions

### Chapters 20-23: Law Codes
- **20:1-17** - Ten Commandments (base text: possibly E, but redacted)
- **20:18-23:15a** - Covenant Code (E source)
- **23:16-33** - Festival laws and promises (E)
- **24:1-2, 24:9-11** - J (covenant ceremony, intimate encounter)
- **24:3-8** - E (alternative covenant ceremony)
- **24:12-14** - J (Moses enters cloud)
- **24:15-18** - P (glory visible)

### Chapters 25-31: Tabernacle Instructions (Primarily P)
- **25:1-27:19** - P (detailed tabernacle, furnishings, altar, court)
- **28:1-29:37** - P (priestly garments and ordination)
- **29:38-42a** - P (daily offerings)
- **30:1-31:18a** - P (incense altar, laver, anointing oil, incense recipe, artisans)

### Chapters 32-34: Golden Calf and Covenant Renewal
- **32:1-6, 32:15-24** - Mixed (calf made)
- **32:7-14** - J (Moses intercedes)
- **32:25-29** - J (Levite action)
- **32:30-35** - Mixed (atonement)
- **33:1-6** - J (covenant renewal offered)
- **33:7-11** - E (tent of meeting)
- **33:12-23** - J (Moses' intimate request)
- **34:1-9** - Mixed
- **34:10-27** - J (covenant renewal and religious calendar)
- **34:28** - E/Redactor (40 days)
- **34:29-35** - P (Moses' shining face)

### Chapters 35-40: Tabernacle Construction (Primarily P)
- **35:1-36:1** - P (sabbath and construction materials)
- **36:2-40:38** - P (construction narrative, inventory, erection, and glory filling)

## LEVITICUS - Complete Breakdown

### Structure: Virtually Entirely Priestly (P)
Leviticus is almost exclusively the Priestly source with minimal input from other sources. It represents P's legal and ritual system.

**Source Attribution by Chapter:**
- **1:1-7:38** - P (Burnt, grain, peace, sin, guilt offerings)
- **8:1-10:20** - P (Priestly ordination and duty)
- **11:1-15:33** - P (Purity laws: clean/unclean animals, disease, discharges)
- **16:1-34** - P (Day of Atonement ritual)
- **17:1-27:34** - P (Blood laws, sexual prohibitions, festivals, sabbath, jubilee, vows, tithes)

**Minimal Non-P Material:**
- A few R (Redactor) harmonizing additions and cross-references
- The Holiness Code (H) within chapters 17-26 is considered a secondary layer within P

## NUMBERS - Complete Breakdown

### Structure: P Dominant (60-70%), with J (10-15%) and E (10-15%)
Numbers is characterized by P's extensive census lists and legal materials, with J and E narratives woven throughout.

### Chapters 1-10: Census and Organization (Primarily P)
- **1:1-54** - P (census of military-eligible men)
- **2:1-34** - P (camp arrangement)
- **3:1-51** - P (Levitical census and redemption)
- **4:1-49** - P (Levite service assignments)
- **5:1-6:27** - P (purification, restitution, Nazirite vows, priestly blessing)
- **7:1-89** - P (dedication offerings)
- **8:1-26** - P (lampstand, Levite dedication)
- **9:1-14** - P (second Passover regulation)
- **10:1-10** - P (silver trumpets)
- **10:11-28** - P (departure from Sinai)

### Chapters 11-14: Wilderness Complaints and Spy Narrative (Mixed)
- **11:1-3** - Mixed (fire complaint)
- **11:4-6** - J (people murmur for meat)
- **11:7-9** - E/Mixed (manna description)
- **11:10-12** - E (Moses' complaint)
- **11:13-14a** - Mixed
- **11:14b-15** - E
- **11:16-20** - Mixed (elders plan)
- **11:21-24a** - J
- **11:24b-30** - E (Spirit on seventy elders)
- **11:31-34** - J (quail provided)
- **12:1-16** - E (Miriam's leprosy for Aaron/Moses criticism)
- **13:1-33** - Multiple sources:
  - **13:1-17a** - P (spy commission)
  - **13:17b-20** - J (spy instructions, reconnaissance)
  - **13:21-24** - J (journabula)
  - **13:25-26a** - P
  - **13:26b-31** - J (report)
  - **13:32** - P (alternative report)
  - **13:33** - J (giants mention)
- **14:1-45** - Complex narrative:
  - **14:1a** - P
  - **14:1b-10** - Mixed (people murmur, J/E)
  - **14:11-25** - J (God's judgment/forgiveness)
  - **14:26-38** - P (legal consequences)
  - **14:39-45** - J (failed attempt to enter Canaan)

### Chapters 15-19: Laws and Rituals (Primarily P)
- **15:1-16** - P (grain/drink offerings)
- **15:17-21** - P (first fruits)
- **15:22-41** - P (sin offering for errors, sabbath-breaker stoning)
- **16:1-50** - Complex rebellion narrative:
  - **16:1-7a** - P (Korah's assembly)
  - **16:2-11** - J/E (core rebellion)
  - **16:12-15** - E (Dathan/Abiram)
  - **16:16-35** - Mixed (judgment)
  - **16:36-50** - P (aftermath and plague)
- **17:1-13** - P (Aaron's rod/budding Levite confirmation)
- **18:1-32** - P (Levite and priestly portions, tithes)
- **19:1-22** - P (red heifer purification ritual)

### Chapters 20-21: Wilderness Wanderings (Mixed)
- **20:1-13** - Multiple sources (Meribah water crisis):
  - **20:1a** - E introduction
  - **20:2-13** - Mixed J/E/P
- **20:14-21** - E (Edom refuses passage)
- **20:22-29** - P (Aaron's death at Mount Hor)
- **21:1-3** - J (victory over Arad)
- **21:4-9** - E (serpents and bronze serpent)
- **21:10-11** - P (itinerary)
- **21:12-20** - E/Mixed (journey stages)
- **21:21-32** - E (victory over Sihon)

### Chapters 22-25: Balaam Narrative and Covenant (Mostly J)
- **22:2-41** - J (Balak summons Balaam)
- **23:1-24:25** - J (Balaam's oracles)
- **25:1-18** - Mixed:
  - **25:1-2a** - E (Moabite seduction)
  - **25:3a** - J (Baal-Peor)
  - **25:3b-4** - J (Israelite judgment)
  - **25:6-8a** - J (Phinehas' act)
  - **25:10-18** - P (Phinehas reward and covenant)

### Chapters 26-36: Final Regulations and Allotments (Primarily P)
- **26:1-65** - P (second census, Deut. census)
- **27:1-23** - P (women inherit, Joshua succeeds Moses)
- **28:1-29:40** - P (festival/offering calendar in detail)
- **30:1-16** - P/Mixed (vow regulations)
- **31:1-54** - P (Midian war and war booty division)
- **32:1-42** - Mixed (Reuben/Gad/Manasseh settlement)
- **33:1-56** - P (detailed itinerary from Egypt to Jordan)
- **34:1-35:34** - P (Canaan boundaries, Levitical cities, cities of refuge)
- **36:1-13** - P (women's inheritance clarified)

## DEUTERONOMY - Complete Breakdown

### Structure: Predominantly Deuteronomist (D) ~80-85%, with P (5-10%) and E (5-10%)
Deuteronomy is distinguished by its consistent theological voice and rhetorical style. The D source is essentially the entire book, with some P appendices.

### Chapters 1-3: Historical Retrospective (Deuteronomist D)
- **1:1-3** - D frame (P note in 1:3)
- **1:4-46** - D (Moses' review of wilderness journeys)
- **2:1-3:29** - D (conquest of Transjordan)

### Chapters 4-11: Exhortations and Covenant (Deuteronomist D)
- **4:1-40** - D (warnings and exhortations)
- **4:41-43** - Prosaic addition (cities of refuge)
- **4:44-49** - Introduction to law code
- **5:1-33** - D (Ten Commandments repeated, Shema preparation)
- **6:1-9:29** - D (extended exhortations about Torah, covenant love, warnings)

### Chapters 10-12: Central Sanctuary and Centralization Laws (Deuteronomist D)
- **10:1-11** - D (covenant renewal, Levite role)
- **10:12-11:32** - D (exhortations about covenant keeping and reward)
- **12:1-28** - D (central sanctuary law - radical reform)

### Chapters 13-18: Laws and Offices (Deuteronomist D)
- **13:1-18** - D (warnings against false prophets/idolatry)
- **14:1-21** - D (dietary laws - simplified relative to P)
- **14:22-29** - D (tithe laws)
- **15:1-18** - D (sabbatical year and slavery release)
- **16:1-17** - D (festivals - three pilgrimage festivals)
- **16:18-17:20** - D (judges, king regulations)
- **18:1-8** - D (Levite support)
- **18:9-22** - D (false prophets and true prophet criteria)

### Chapters 19-27: Miscellaneous Laws (Deuteronomist D)
- **19:1-21** - D (cities of refuge, witnesses)
- **20:1-20** - D (holy war laws)
- **21:1-25:19** - D (miscellaneous laws: murder, family, slaves, mourning, etc.)
- **26:1-15** - D (first fruits offering and tithe declaration)
- **26:16-19** - D (covenant statement)
- **27:1-26** - D (Mount Ebal ceremony and curses - Dtr1)

### Chapter 28: Blessings and Curses (Deuteronomist D)
- **28:1-68** - D (extensive blessings and curses for covenant obedience/disobedience)

### Chapters 29-30: Covenant Renewal (Deuteronomist D)
- **29:1-28** - D (covenant renewal ceremony)
- **29:29** - D (hidden/revealed matters)
- **30:1-10** - D (future repentance and restoration)
- **30:11-14** - D (law's accessibility)
- **30:15-20** - D (choice between life and death)

### Chapters 31-34: Final Leadership and Death (Mixed with P/E)
- **31:1-8** - D (charge to Joshua)
- **31:9-13** - D (Torah reading instruction)
- **31:14-15** - E (God/Joshua commissioning)
- **31:16-22** - D (covenant breaking predicted)
- **31:23** - E (Joshua commissioning)
- **31:24-29** - D (law deposition and witness)
- **32:1-47** - D (Song of Moses - ancient poetry incorporated)
- **32:48-52** - P (notice of Moses' impending death)
- **33:1-29** - J (Blessing of Moses - ancient poetry)
- **34:1a** - P (Moses ascends)
- **34:1b-4** - Mixed (death on Pisgah, view of land)
- **34:5-6** - E/Mixed (death and burial mystery)
- **34:7-9** - P (statement of full vigor, Joshua filled with wisdom)
- **34:10-12** - Redactional conclusion (unmatched prophet)

## Key Observations

### Exodus
- **Narrative framework:** Primarily J and E with P providing alternative accounts
- **Ritual focus:** Chapters 25-40 increasingly P, reflecting P's tabernacle theology
- **Legal codes:** E's Covenant Code (20-23) provides foundational laws

### Leviticus
- **Unique composition:** Almost entirely Priestly
- **Purpose:** Systematic legal and ritual instruction
- **No competing narratives:** Different sources don't offer alternative accounts here

### Numbers
- **Census and law:** Heavy P presence with tabernacle materials
- **Narrative variety:** J and E offer stories of wilderness complaints and rebellion
- **Mixed theology:** Demonstrates how law (P) and narrative (J/E) were combined

### Deuteronomy
- **D dominance:** Consistent theological voice throughout
- **Rhetorical style:** Sermons, exhortations, covenant renewal framework
- **Time period:** Scholars associate D with 7th century BCE court/prophetic reform
