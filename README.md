# Bible Vault 📖

Your Personal Torah Study Companion - An interactive platform for studying the Five Books of Moses with visual timelines, character insights, and personal note-taking.

## Features

✨ **Multi-Translation Support**
- English Standard Version (ESV)
- King James Version (KJV)
- Bulgarian Bible (1940)
- Hebrew: Westminster Leningrad Codex (WLCa)
- Greek: Septuagint (LXX)
- Easy translation switching with dropdown selector
- Saves your translation preference

📅 **Interactive Historical Timeline**
- 22 major biblical characters from Creation to Moses
- Visual lifespan overlaps showing who lived when
- Click any character to jump to their first appearance
- Zoom and scroll through 2500+ years of history
- Smart highlighting based on current chapter

📝 **Personal Study Tools**
- Click any verse to add personal notes
- Notes persist in browser storage
- Visual indicators show which verses have notes
- Highlight important passages

🎨 **Beautiful Design**
- Warm, earthy color palette for comfortable reading
- Responsive layout for desktop and mobile
- Smooth animations and transitions
- Collapsible timeline drawer

## How to Use

1. Select a book from the dropdown (Genesis, Exodus, etc.)
2. Choose a chapter to read
3. Click on any verse to add personal notes
4. Explore the timeline at the bottom to see character lifespans
5. Click timeline characters to jump to their story
